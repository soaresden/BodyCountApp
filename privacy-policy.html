Bodycount privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · Version française

Bodycount is a personal journal. It was built so that your data never leaves your phone.

We don't want your data

That's on purpose: what's in your journal is none of our business. Bodycount does not process any personal data on its own behalf.

  • No sign-up: no account, no Google sign-in, no email address required.
  • No collection: no server, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools. The app blocks every connection: your logbook can't leave your phone. The only connection is made by the Google Play Billing library, used for the optional Bodycount Ultimate purchase; it never sees your logbook.
  • Purchases: the Ultimate subscription or purchase is handled entirely by Google Play (payment, free trial, cancellation). Google only shares the order details we need (order number, product, country), never your logbook data.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, with anyone.

Everything is local and encrypted

The entries, notes, dates and settings you create are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, and encrypted (AES-256) with a key protected by your passcode. They are excluded from Google's automatic backups. Nobody but you can read them, not even the developer, who has no access to them.

Nothing leaves the app without you: you alone decide what you share, and with whom.

Your backup, your key

If you export a backup, the file is encrypted with a password only you know. We don't have it and can't recover it: without it, the file is unreadable to anyone.

You choose where to save it. We recommend keeping it somewhere of your own (your personal cloud such as Google Drive, iCloud or Proton Drive, a USB stick, your computer): since it's encrypted, it reveals nothing even if copied, and it lets you get your journal back if you change or lose your phone.

Strictly personal use

Bodycount is for adults only (18+). Entries may be about other people: they are only for your personal journal, stay on your phone and are never sent to anyone. By using the app, you agree not to share or publish this information, to write down the minimum (a first name or nickname is enough) and to delete an entry if the person concerned asks you to.

Permissions

  • Contacts (optional): when you tap “Pick from my contacts”, Android opens its own picker; Bodycount only receives the name and number of the contact you choose (no access to the rest of your address book). They are encrypted in the app and only used for the Call / SMS / WhatsApp buttons, which open the matching app without sending anything on your behalf.
  • Biometrics (optional): to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face. This data stays managed by Android; Bodycount never has access to it.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback.
  • Notifications (optional): a reminder 2 h before a date you logged (“Hey you! You’ve got a date!”) and the day after (“So, champ?”), with no name or details. Can be turned off in settings, off in discreet icon mode.

Your rights

Since everything stays on your phone, you exercise your rights yourself, at any time:

  • access and correction: directly in the app;
  • portability: Settings › Export a backup;
  • erasure: delete an entry, Settings › Erase everything, or uninstall the app. Without a backup, the data is then permanently lost.

As the developer holds no data, they can't access, recover or erase it on your behalf.
